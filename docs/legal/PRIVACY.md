# OmaPointii privacy notice

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

This notice explains what the OmaPointii pilot does with personal data. It is a plain-language summary, not legal advice.

## What we collect

- **Account:** email address, name, language, sign-in identifiers.
- **Loyalty:** points earned and redeemed, rewards, store memberships, visits.
- **Preferences:** store-news consent, in-app notification preference.
- **Feedback:** the message you send and the surface it came from.
- **Technical:** error reports from Sentry, if the operator has enabled it.

We do not collect payment card data. A store enters the purchase amount; OmaPointii never sees the card.

## Why we use it

- To run the loyalty service, show balances, and let stores award and redeem points.
- To send the sign-in code you ask for.
- To answer support and feedback.
- To prevent fraud and abuse.
- To meet legal obligations.

The legal bases are performing the service you asked for, your consent for store news, and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

## Who sees it

- **The store where you earn or redeem** sees the transactions at that store, your name/email as needed for the till, and your points balance there. It does not see your other stores.
- **Service providers** process data for us: Supabase (database and authentication), Render (API hosting), Vercel (web hosting), Sentry (error reports, if enabled), and the email provider used for sign-in codes.
- We do not sell personal data.

## How long we keep it

- The points ledger is kept while the service runs so store totals stay consistent.
- If you delete your account, we remove your name, email, phone, codes and store consents, and keep only anonymised ledger totals.
- A pending customer record that is never claimed is deleted after one year.

## Your rights

You can:

- **Download your data** in Settings → Download my data.
- **Delete your account** in Settings → Delete my account. This needs a fresh email code.
- Ask for correction or restriction, or object to processing.
- Complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman.

Contact: **privacy@omapointii.store**.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is limited by role, and staff accounts need a second factor. We keep audit records of sensitive actions.

## Changes

We will update this notice as the pilot develops. The current version is shown in the app.
