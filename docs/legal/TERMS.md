# OmaPointii pilot terms of use

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

These terms cover the OmaPointii pilot. They are a plain-language summary for the pilot stores and their customers, not legal advice.

## 1. The service

OmaPointii is a loyalty service operated for participating stores. A store sets its own earning rules, rewards and offers. OmaPointii provides the app, the till and the records.

## 2. Points

- Points have **no cash value** and cannot be exchanged for cash.
- Points are held **per store**. A balance at one store cannot be spent at another.
- Points expire **three months after they are earned**. The oldest points are used first.
- A store may change its earning rules and rewards for future purchases. Changes do not rewrite past entries.
- A reward is subject to the store's stock and its daily limit, if it has one.

## 3. Your account

- You need a working email address and a sign-in code.
- Keep your code private. Tell us at once if someone else uses your account.
- You must not use the service for fraud, abuse, or to interfere with another customer or store.

## 4. Availability

The pilot is provided as-is. We aim to keep it available, but we do not promise uninterrupted service. A store may pause or close its loyalty programme.

## 5. Changes and ending use

We may update these terms as the pilot develops. The current version is always shown in the app. You can delete your account in Settings at any time. A store may stop using OmaPointii; its points then follow that store's own notice.
