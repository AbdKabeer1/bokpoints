1. Purpose and conventions
This document says exactly what BOKPOINTS must do. The PRD explains why and for
whom; this FRD explains what the system does, screen by screen and rule by rule. It is
written so that one builder, or an AI coding agent working from AGENT.md, can implement
from it.
Detail level: the MVP is specified in full. v1.1 and v2 are outlined only and will be detailed
before their phase begins.
Requirement IDs: FR-<MODULE>-<number> , for example FR-PTS-3 .
Priority words:
MUST: required for the phase it is tagged with.
SHOULD: strongly wanted; cut only if time forces it.
COULD: nice to have.
Phase tags: [MVP], [v1.1], [v2]. A requirement with no tag is [MVP].
Modules: AUTH (accounts and sign-in), CUS (customer dashboard), STO (store
dashboard), ADM (admin panel), PTS (points engine), CRD (credentials), BIL (billing), NTF
(notifications), API (API and integrations), AUD (audit and fraud), PRV (privacy and

Rules that hold across the model:
The ledger is append-only. Entries are never edited or deleted. There are no reversals: a
manual adjustment (see 4.5) corrects a mistake, and only an admin can apply a fraud
clawback (see 4.6).
Money is stored as whole cents in euros. Points are whole numbers.
A customer's balance at a store is the sum of that customer's ledger entries for that
store, expiry entries included.
Points belong to the store that issued them and cannot be used elsewhere.
Points expire 3 months after the day they were earned. The oldest points are used first.
A balance never goes below zero, except through an admin fraud clawback.

4. Key flows (MVP)
4.1 Admin onboards a store
1. Admin creates the store record: name, business ID, address, contact person, email, VAT
details, plan.
2. System sends an invitation email to the owner with a secure link.
3. Owner accepts, sets up sign-in, and lands in the store dashboard in setup mode.
4. Owner completes the store profile, sets the points rule, and adds staff.
5. Subscription starts according to the plan (trial or immediate billing).
6. Store status becomes Active once setup and billing are complete.
Store status lifecycle: Invited, Setup, Active, Paused (from day 14 of a failed payment),
Suspended (from day 30), Offboarded. A reminder goes to the owner on day 7 without
changing the status (see FR-BIL-4).
4.2 Customer signs up
1. Customer installs the app from the Apple App Store or Google Play, or opens the web
app, and enters an email address.
2. Customer verifies with a one-time code or link sent by email.
3. Customer accepts the terms and privacy notice, and chooses a language. A phone
number can be added but is not verified in the MVP.
4. Customer lands on the dashboard with a personal QR code and an empty store list.
4.3 Cashier awards points (core flow)
1. Cashier opens the Cashier screen (an installable web app) and signs in once per shift
or device.
2. Customer shows the QR code in the app (later: taps an NFC card). The QR code works
with or without a connection.
3. Cashier scans it with the device camera.
4. The device checks the code. Online, the system resolves the credential to the
customer and shows the first name and the current balance at this store. Offline, the
device verifies the code on its own and shows the first name only; no balance is shown.
5. Cashier enters the purchase amount in euros.

6. The screen shows the amount and the points it will award, and the cashier confirms.
There is no undo after this step.
7. Online: the system writes a ledger entry and updates the balance, and both screens
show the confirmation. Offline: the device saves the award in a local queue, shows
"Saved offline", and sends it when the connection returns; the customer's balance
updates after that sync.
If this is the customer's first purchase at this store, the system also creates the customer-
store link when the award is recorded. The customer sees a first-time notice the next time
they open the app, with the store's marketing consent shown as off until the customer
opts in.
4.4 Cashier registers a customer without the app [MVP]
1. Cashier enters the customer's email address.
2. System sends the customer an invitation to create an account and claim any points.
SMS is not used in the MVP.
3. Points earned before sign-up are held on a pending account and become visible once
the customer verifies the account.
4. Pending points expire after 3 months like any other points. If the customer never
claims the account, the pending record, including the email address, is deleted after 1
year.
4.5 Manual adjustment
1. Owner or manager opens a customer in the store customer list.
2. They choose Add or Remove points, enter the amount and a required reason. Removing
points cannot take the balance below zero.
3. System writes an adjustment ledger entry and an audit log entry.
4.6 Fraud clawback (admin only)
1. After a fraud case is confirmed, the admin opens the customer-store record.
2. Admin chooses Fraud clawback, enters the points to remove and a case note. This is
the only action that can take a balance below zero.
3. System writes a clawback ledger entry, marks the customer-store link as under fraud
review, and writes an audit log entry. Later earnings first offset a negative balance
(proposed).

4.7 Redemption [v1.1]
1. Customer chooses a reward in the app or asks at the till.
2. Cashier scans the customer, selects the reward, and confirms.
3. System checks the balance, writes a redeem entry using the oldest points first, and
shows the new balance.
4.8 Offline awards and sync [MVP]
1. The Cashier screen keeps working without a connection. It holds the store's points
rule, the list of revoked credentials, and the keys it needs to check QR codes.
2. Each offline award is saved in an encrypted local queue with its scan time, amount,
points, rule version, device ID and idempotency key.
3. When the connection returns, the device sends the queue automatically and retries
until every item is confirmed.
4. The server accepts an award if, at the scan time, the credential was valid, the store was
not suspended, the QR proof had not been used before, and the amount was within the
offline limits.
5. An accepted award is written to the ledger with its original scan time, and the
customer's balance updates.
6. A rejected award is not written to the ledger. It appears in the store's rejected-awards
list with the reason, and the owner can add points manually if the purchase was
genuine.

FR-ADM-8 (SHOULD): A support notes field on each store and customer, visible only

5.5 PTS: points engine
FR-PTS-1 (MUST): Points = purchase amount in euros times the store's points-per-euro
rate, rounded using the store's rounding rule (default: round down to a whole number).
FR-PTS-2 (MUST): If the amount is below the store's minimum purchase, award zero
points and tell the cashier why.
FR-PTS-3 (MUST): If the result exceeds the store's maximum per transaction, award
the maximum and show that it was capped.
FR-PTS-4 (MUST): Each award request carries an idempotency key, so a retry, a double
tap, or a resend after an offline period never awards twice.
FR-PTS-5 (MUST): Write the ledger entry and update the balance in one atomic step; if
either fails, neither is saved.
FR-PTS-6 (MUST): Reject online awards for suspended or offboarded stores and for
revoked credentials, with a clear message. Offline awards are judged at sync on the
state at the scan time (FR-PTS-11).

FR-PTS-7 (SHOULD): Warn the cashier when the same customer received an award at
this store for the same amount within a short window (default: 2 minutes), and require
confirmation. The check also works offline, on that device only.
FR-PTS-8 (MUST): There are no reversals. A balance can never go below zero, except
through an admin fraud clawback.
FR-PTS-9 (v1.1): Redemption checks the balance, uses the oldest points first, writes a
redeem entry, and rejects it if points are insufficient.
FR-PTS-10 (MUST): Points expire 3 months after the day they were earned. Each earn
entry records its expiry date and its remaining points, and a daily job writes an expiry
entry for each batch that has run out of time.
FR-PTS-11 (MUST): An offline award keeps the time of the scan. Its expiry date is
counted from that time, and it is accepted at sync if the credential, the store status
and the offline limits were valid at that time.
FR-PTS-12 (MUST): A one-time QR proof can be used once per store. A second use is
rejected, including when two devices send it at sync.
Worked example: the store rule is 1 point per euro, rounded down. The cashier enters
23.80 euros. The customer receives 23 points.
5.6 CRD: credentials
FR-CRD-1 (MUST): Every customer has an app QR credential created at sign-up.
FR-CRD-2 (MUST): The QR code can be checked without a connection. Proposed
design: it carries a credential signed by the platform (customer, credential and the
phone's public key, valid for about 7 days and refreshed automatically whenever the
app is online) plus a one-time proof created by the phone, valid for about one minute.
The cashier device checks both locally. The final design is set in the architecture work.
FR-CRD-3 (MUST): The system resolves any credential type to the same customer ID,
so awarding logic does not depend on how the customer was identified.
FR-CRD-4 (SHOULD): A customer or admin can mark a credential lost or revoked.
Online checks reject it at once; offline devices learn of it at their next sync, so the
credential validity period limits the exposure.
FR-CRD-5 (v1.1): Physical NFC card credential: stores only an opaque identifier, never a
balance; cryptographically protected against cloning; linked to a customer by
activation in the app or by an admin. Offline checking of cards needs card keys on the
reader side and is designed in that phase.

5.7 BIL: billing
FR-BIL-1 (MUST): Each store has a monthly subscription with a plan, a start date, and
an optional trial.
FR-BIL-2 (MUST): Payments are processed by an external payment provider;
BOKPOINTS stores no card numbers.
FR-BIL-3 (MUST): Issue an invoice for each billing period that shows the correct Finnish

FR-BIL-4 (MUST): Failed payments follow this schedule, counted from the first failed

14, and the store Suspended at the end of the first month (day 30). Paying at any point
restores the store at once.
FR-BIL-5 (MUST): A Paused store cannot create or change campaigns, rewards,
products or points rules, but can still award points. A Suspended store cannot award
points and its dashboard is read-only except for billing. Customers can always see their
balances and history, and points still expire.
FR-BIL-6 (SHOULD): Owner can change plan or cancel; cancellation takes effect at the
end of the paid period.
FR-BIL-7 (COULD): Charge separately for NFC cards.
5.8 NTF: notifications
FR-NTF-1 (MUST): In the MVP, transactional messages go by email only: verification
codes, invitations, security notices and billing reminders. SMS is not used.
FR-NTF-2 (MUST): Customer notices (a new award, points expiring soon) appear in an
in-app notice area.
FR-NTF-3 (v1.1): Push notifications and store alerts go only to customers who opted in
for that store, and every message gives an easy way to opt out.
FR-NTF-4 (v1.1): Customers can control channels (push, email) and mute a single store.
FR-NTF-5 (v1.1): Limit how many marketing messages a customer can receive per store
per period (limit to be decided).
FR-NTF-6 (v2): SMS as an extra channel once a cost plan exists. SMS fees are normally
billed to the sender, so BOKPOINTS would carry them unless they are passed on to
stores.

5.9 API: API and integrations
FR-API-1 (MUST): The Cashier screen uses the same internal API as any future
integration: identify customer, award points (including batches of offline awards), and
get balance.
FR-API-2 (MUST): Every API call is authenticated, scoped to one store, and permission-
checked.
FR-API-3 (MUST): The API is versioned and documented from the start.
FR-API-4 (v2): Store-level API keys and webhooks for POS integrations, with rate limits
and per-key revocation.
FR-API-5 (v2): Webhooks notify a store system when points are awarded or reversed.
5.10 AUD: audit and fraud
FR-AUD-1 (MUST): Record in the audit log: store creation and status changes, staff
changes, rule changes, manual adjustments, fraud clawbacks, credential and device
changes, rejected offline awards, and admin support actions.
FR-AUD-2 (MUST): Audit entries cannot be edited or deleted through the product.
FR-AUD-3 (SHOULD): Flag unusual patterns for review: many awards by one cashier in
a short time, repeated large amounts, many adjustments, or repeated failed scans.
FR-AUD-4 (SHOULD): Rate-limit scanning and awarding per device and per cashier.
FR-AUD-5 (SHOULD): Limit the exposure of offline use: cap the amount per offline
award and the number of awards waiting per device, and flag devices whose clock
differs from server time.
5.11 PRV: privacy and consent
FR-PRV-1 (MUST): Show the terms and privacy notice at customer sign-up and record
the version accepted and the time.
FR-PRV-2 (MUST): Marketing consent is separate from the terms, off by default,
recorded per store, and can be withdrawn at any time.
FR-PRV-3 (MUST): A customer can export their data and delete their account. On
deletion, personal data is removed and ledger entries are kept in anonymized form for
store accounting.
FR-PRV-4 (MUST): A store can see only its own customers' data and cannot see other
stores' balances or history.

8. Non-functional requirements
FR-NFR-1 (MUST) Speed: a scan-to-confirmation round trip should feel immediate.
Proposed target: under 3 seconds on a normal mobile connection, measured at the
95th percentile. An offline confirmation appears on the device within about one

11. Decisions made and open points
Open points
1. Expiry meaning (confirmed): this FRD reads "every 3 months" as each batch of points
expiring 3 months after it was earned.
2. "Pause" (confirmed): this FRD defines Paused as no changes to campaigns, rewards,
products or rules, while awarding continues.

adjustment (never below zero).

a privacy review of the lawful basis.
